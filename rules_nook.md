#### **1. Introduction & Objective**

**VerbaPix** is a sentence-making card game that is part of the Master Grammar Series. The goal is to put different cards together to make grammatically correct sentences by following rules to match colors and symbols.

*

**Set 1 Focus:** Past, present, and future simple tenses in affirmative and negative forms.

*

**Set 2 Focus:** Adds question forms and more complex vocabulary.

#### **2. Card Types & Identification**

There are 60 cards per set. Each card has a reference code in the bottom left corner (e.g., N1a).

*

**N (Noun):** Person, animal, or thing (Subject or Object).

*

**V (Verb):** The action.

*

**L (Location):** Where the action happens.

*

**P (Preposition):** Connects the verb to the object.

*

**H (Helping Verb):** Controls the tense/time of the sentence (e.g., *will, did*).

*

**T (Time):** When the action happens.

*

**A (Adjective):** Describes a noun.

*

**AV (Adverb):** Modifies an adjective or verb (Frequency or Degree).

*

**E (Extra):** Relative clauses adding information to a noun.

*

**C (Conjunction):** Joins parts of a sentence (e.g., *and, or*).

*

**W (Wild):** Can be used as any card type.

*

**PvP:** Player vs Player action cards.

#### **3. Core Mechanics: Matching Rules**

To play a valid card, you must match **Colours** and **Symbols**.

**A. Matching Colours (Syntax)**
Colors on the bottom left and bottom right of each card indicate the correct syntax order.

* **Subject:** Noun (Purple/Green/Red bars)  Verb.
* **Verb:** Green bar  Preposition (Red bar).
*

**Object:** Preposition  Noun (White bar).

*

**Time/Location:** Can be placed at the beginning or end of the sentence to emphasize those words.

**B. Matching Symbols (Grammar)**

*

**Smiley Faces (Number):** Match singular (one face) or plural (two faces) Nouns to the corresponding Verb form.

*

*Example:* A card with one smiley face (singular subject) matches a verb form with one smiley face (e.g., "sings").

*

**Tense Squares:** Match the colored square on a Time card (e.g., "Yesterday") to the matching square on the Verb card.

*

*Example:* "Yesterday" (Black Square 7) matches "Sang" (Black Square).

* **Adjective Icons:** Adjectives have symbols indicating if they describe People (Person icon), Animals (Paw icon), or Places (House icon). You must match these to the Noun/Location type.

#### **4. Scoring**

*

**Card Points:** Each card has a point value on the top right corner.

* **Bonus Points:** Bottom of the card lists specific combinations that award extra points (e.g., "Noun + Verb"). Bonus points apply regardless of tense or plurality.

*

**Full Hand Bonus:** If a player uses all 7 cards in their hand in the Standard Game, they get +5 points.

#### **5. Game Modes**

**Mode A: Standard Game (Competitive)**

*

**Setup:** Deal 7 cards to each player.

*

**Objective:** Highest total points for one sentence made from up to 7 cards.

* **Gameplay Rounds:**
*

**Round 1:** Discard 1, 2, or 3 cards  Take new cards.

*

**Round 2:** Discard 1, 2, or 3 cards  Take new cards.

*

**Round 3:** Discard 1, 2, or 3 cards  Take new cards.

*

**End:** Players lay down their sentences, read them aloud, and tally points.

**Mode B: 5/4 Split (Competitive)**

*

**Objective:** Make a sentence using 5 cards from your hand + 4 cards from the middle (Community Pool).

* **Setup:** Deal 2 cards to each player. Place 1 card face-up in the middle.

* **Round 1:**
*

**State:** 1 Card in Middle, 2 Cards in Hand.

*

**Action:** Discard 1 card, Take 1 card.

* **Round 2:**
*

**State:** Deal 1 card to Middle (Total 2), Deal 1 card to Hand (Total 3).

*

**Action:** Discard 1 card, Take 1 card.

* **Round 3:**
*

**State:** Deal 1 card to Middle (Total 3), Deal 1 card to Hand (Total 4).

*

**Action:** Discard 1 card, Take 1 card.

* **Round 4:**
*

**State:** Deal 1 card to Middle (Total 4), Deal 1 card to Hand (Total 5).

*

**Action:** Discard **up to 2 cards**, Take **up to 2 cards**.

*

**Scoring:** Create a sentence using up to 7 cards total (combining hand and middle cards).

**Mode C: Co-op Game (Cooperative)**

* **Objective:** Build one long sentence together. The winner is the last player to successfully add a card.

*

**Setup:** Deal 3 cards to each player (4 if 2 players).

* **Rules:**
* First card played must be a Noun.

* On a turn, a player adds 1 card to the sentence OR discards 1 and draws 1.

* Every time a card is added, the player must read the full sentence aloud.

* **Locking:** Cards like Prepositions and Wilds are "Locked." Nouns/Verbs can be replaced if they match for bonus points.

#### **6. Advanced Rules**

* **PvP Cards:** 4-6 cards added to the deck. Example: "Swap" (trade a card with a player), "Double Points" (x2 for one card), "Time Warp" (change scoring rules for tenses).

* **Wild Cards:** Used when a player lacks a Noun or Verb. The card scores 0 points but allows the sentence to be completed.
